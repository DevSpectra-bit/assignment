Mobile App

Feature Backend Beta

We have started work on the new Mobile App for Assignment Tracker! This app will allow you to track your assignments on the go, receive notifications, and manage your tasks more efficiently. The app is currently in beta, and we welcome feedback from early users.

How it works

The Mobile App connects to your Assignment Tracker account and syncs your assignments in real-time. You can view your tasks, mark them as complete, and receive push notifications for upcoming deadlines. The app is designed to be lightweight and user-friendly, ensuring that you can manage your assignments with ease.

  • Real-time Sync: Any changes made on the web app will reflect on the mobile app instantly.
  • Push Notifications: Get notified about upcoming deadlines and important updates.
  • Offline Mode: Access your assignments even when you don't have an internet connection.
  • Feedback: We encourage users to provide feedback to help us improve the app. You can submit feedback directly through the app's settings.

The Mobile App is currently in beta, and we are actively working on adding more features and improving the user experience. Stay tuned for updates and new releases!
